# Shipova Support

Shipova is an App Store screenshot design studio for macOS. This page answers the most common questions. If you need anything else, email **[dylan.sola@icloud.com](mailto:dylan.sola@icloud.com)** and you'll get a reply from the developer.

## Getting started

1. Open **Templates** and choose a design for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch or Mac.
2. Choose **Import** (or drag images onto the window) to add your own app screenshots.
3. Edit the headline, colors and device angle in the inspector.
4. Choose **Export** to save PNG or JPEG files at App Store sizes.

The built-in guide is under **Help > Shipova Guide**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Shipova upload my screenshots to App Store Connect?**
No. Export saves image files to a folder you choose. You then add them to your app's page in App Store Connect yourself.

**What is free, and what does Shipova Pro add?**
Designing and exporting iPhone screenshots is free, including the 3D studio and 12 starter designs, with no watermark. Shipova Pro adds iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and iPhone Duo, every design and connected panoramas, extra languages, one-click color palettes, resizing to Apple's sizes and the Fastlane folder arrangement. You can try Pro features in the editor before buying; exporting them needs Pro.

**How do I restore a purchase on another Mac or after reinstalling?**
Choose **Shipova > Restore Purchases**, or open the Shipova Pro window and click **Restore Purchases**. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I cancel or change my subscription?**
Open the App Store, click your name, then **Account Settings > Subscriptions > Manage**. You can also use **Help > Manage Subscription** in Shipova. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

**I bought Lifetime but I still have a subscription.**
A Lifetime purchase does not cancel an existing subscription. Cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings so it does not renew.

**How do I request a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Where are my projects stored?**
On your Mac only. Shipova autosaves your current session, and **File > Save Project** creates a portable `.shipova` file that includes your images. Earlier sessions are under **Help > Show Recovery Projects**.

**Which macOS version do I need?**
macOS 14 or later.

## Reporting a problem

Email **[dylan.sola@icloud.com](mailto:dylan.sola@icloud.com)** with what you were doing, what happened, and your macOS and Shipova versions. Attaching the `.shipova` project helps if the problem is with a particular design.
